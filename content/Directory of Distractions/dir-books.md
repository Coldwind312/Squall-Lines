**_The Discworld Series_** (by Sir Terry Pratchett)

_**The Hitch-hiker's Guide to the Galaxy**_ (by Douglas Adams)  
Consists of five books:  
1. _The Hitch-hiker's Guide to the Galaxy_  
2. _The Restaurant at the End of the Universe_  
3. _Life, the Universe and Everything_  
4. _So Long, and Thanks for All the Fish_  
5. _Mostly Harmless_

There's also a sixth novel, stemming from the fact that Douglas Adams publicly said he wasn't happy with the bleakness of the ending of _Mostly Harmless_, and that he'd like to write a sixth book. But he died before he had chance. And unfortunately those left in charge of his legacy handed the rights to Eoin Colfer, author of the Artemis Fowl books, and he wrote *And Another Thing…*

I'm not saying it's a bad book. I wouldn't know. This is only partially because of purism—the feeling that Douglas Adams’ series belongs to Douglas Adams. I mean it’s not like other series don’t get instalments from other authors than their originators. I think also it’s just that I never really had the motivation by then. _Mostly Harmless_ _did_ end bleak; but it _was_ an ending.
