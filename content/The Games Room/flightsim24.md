Hereafter known as FS24, because it's easier to type, *Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024* is the latest in the popular series of mostly civil flight simulations released under Microsoft's branding.

The evil megacorp has been producing the *Flight Simulator* franchise since buying the first version off Bruce Artwick's SubLogic studio in 1982. The current version is the 2024 version which I imagine will be replaced by a better one in a few minutes to suck as much money as possible out of aviation-obsessed hobbyist nutters like me[^1].
