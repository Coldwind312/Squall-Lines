They say you can learn a lot about someone by the things they enjoy.

At least, they probably say that. Sounds like common sense, really.

With that in mind, though, I would like to offer you a selection of

## FAVES

by which you might better assess just how admirable/reprehensible/unremarkable I am, and maybe find a thing or two you agree on, disagree on, or didn’t know about and now think you might give a try.

Initially this will start out as basic lists of my Top Five Examples, without too much commentary. But in time, I hope to add a bit of detail and reasoning, and it’s more than likely that the lists might expand past five in some cases (I struggle to make my mind up about things, for the most part).

### PREFERRED PLAYS

1. _Death of a Salesman_ - Arthur Miller's 1949 about a travelling salesman facing advancing age and what he perceives as the darkening of his life. It has... an effect.
2. _The Crucible_ - Written by Arthur Miller in 1953 as an allegory for 'McCarthyism'—the paranoid anti-communist insanity gripping US politics at the time (quo magis mutatur, eo magis idem est)—the Crucible is a dramatisation of the most famous histrionic moral panic: the late medieval and early modern witch trials; specifically, the events in Salem in 1692-3.
3. _Life of Pi_ - A young man spends a third of a year stranded on a lifeboat. A tiger may or may not be involved. 
4. _Player Kings_ - Ian McKellan starred as Falstaff in a double-feature Shakespeare's *Henry V* parts 1 and 2.
5. _Mythos: Ragnarök_ - The story of the world-ending battle between the Norse gods and the armies of the frost giants, as told through the medium of professional wrestling.
### MERITORIOUS MUSIC[^2]

I’ve currently got 450 songs on my Liked list on Spotify, so distilling them down to five is a fool’s errand. I will pick five for the sake of the list but treat it as a highlight. None of these lists are really absolute, but this one's even less absolute than that.

1. *El Fusilado*; The Longest Johns (after Chumbawamba). The catchiest song there’s ever been about an execution (failed) by firing squad.
2. *Amaze Amaze Amaze (Life on Erid)*; Daniel Pemberton. From the soundtrack to the magnificent *Project Hail Mary*. A track that puts a spring in your step, even if you’re not in microgravity.
3. *Disease*; Lady Gaga
4. *Taranta*; Ludovico Einaudi. Einaudi is one of those composers whose work you know when you hear it even if you don’t know the exact piece. Taranta is very different from his usual (equally excellent) style. Here he puts reflective aside and gets together with a bunch of other musicians—including an accordionist wait stay with me—aside and sets out to kick some ass.
5. *My Sails Are Set*; Sonya Belousova, Giona Ostinelli. From the TV show One Piece, which is something about anime pirates but in live action? I don’t really know. But this track is beautiful: ‘determined despite everything’ kind of stuff.

### PRIZED PODCASTS[^3]

1. *Astonishing Legends*: Scott Philbrook and Forrest Burgess explore and investigate mysterious and mystical events. Mostly those in the paranormal bracket.
2. *Strange and Unexplained with Daisy Eagan*: Writer and actor Daisy looks into, well, strange and unexplained happenings. Does paranormal, disappearances and true crime.
3. *Ruined with Alison Leiby and Halle Kiefer*: Alison doesn't like horror films. Halle loves them. In this show Halle takes Alison and the listener through a detailed summary of movies while Alison offers commentary and tries to guess Who Will Survive.
4. *Unexplained*: Richard MacLean Smith explores events 
5. *Respect the Dead*: Tagline: 'The podcast where we don't'. Caelan Conrad and Amanda 'Hoots' Hootman examine the lives and of the thankfully departed, and the ways in which their time on Earth made things worse for everybody else.
