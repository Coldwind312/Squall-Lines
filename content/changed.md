Hello there, my *terribly* dear reader. 

Please do come in and make yourself *quite* at home.

Excuse the clutter: I've recently had a bit of a shift-around.

If you've visited Squall Lines before you may have noticed that things are different. It looks different. Things aren't where once they were. Primarily this is because my blog, such as it ever was, has for many years been hosted on WordPress.com, a good solid well-established bloggery platform.

Like many such services WordPress offers a number of pricing tiers and for a long time—probably too long given the very low traffic to my site—I'd maintained one of the paid tiers in order that any visitors I did get wouldn't be subjected to adverts. Because that's very annoying.

But the times they roll on. Mostly they get shittier because of immensely rich old white men deciding to make them so for the rest of us and refusing to let us have communism. And the costs of living are fairly dizzying these days, so other options have to be found.

In the case of this site, such as it is, the solution for now is for me to host it on a GitHub repository and try to keep it small. GitHub aren't particularly grumpy about this, but website hosting isn't the main of the platform so while they're pretty chill, they don't like hosted sites getting *too* massive.

But low traffic means it's viable to keep it here, and massive traffic would make it more financially justifiable to move it back to paid hosting anyway.

Anyway for now it’s here. This means there might not be the amount of images on it that I've always previously tended to use, because images can be one of the things that tend to occupy most storage space and bandwidth, but I may find workarounds for that in time.

There also won't be the common interaction features one might find on a properly developed blog: no comments sections, probably no feeds or subscription or mailing list options, that kind of thing. Again, if I can find workarounds I'll implement them in future if it's practical and within my skills. 

Otherwise if you've a need to comment at me you can find me on Facebook (though I am trying hard to give it up because it's of very little use as anything other than an anger-generating machine). I also have a fairly dead account on Mastodon. And you can always reach me [by email](mailto:coldwindcontact@proton.me).

In the meantime please make yourself at home. Explore the new layout: although links may be in different places I'll likely try to keep much the same general site plan. ("Plan", she says...)

And (because it's an old tradition at this point), I'll Try To Update More Often Too™.
