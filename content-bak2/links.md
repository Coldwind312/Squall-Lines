### **Astronomy and Space**

- [The European Space Agency](https://www.esa.int/): the joint space agency of Europe. But you knew that. They’re one of several space agencies that conspiracy theorists don’t seem to realise exist; or at least will tell you are basically just branches of NASA designed to lull us SHEEPLE or whatever godsdamned nonsense.
- [The National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA)](https://www.nasa.gov/) : the space agency of the United States of America. Offers scientific information about spaceflight and space research, details and logs of missions manned, automated and static, and prevents the SHEEPLE from reaching the Ice Wall around the Edge of the Flat Earth[1](https://squalllines.wordpress.com/?page_id=10919&preview=true#ec1ea7ef-ac39-4f59-ac75-b37134997e38).
- [The UK Space Agency](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/uk-space-agency) : Yes, we've got one, bizarrely enough. We've actually launched two satellites (in 1962 and 1971), though mostly these days our agency works on technology concepts and relies on allies for the actual rocketry.

### **Words and That**

- [Wiktionary](https://en.wiktionary.org/) : the English edition of a comprehensive user-maintained dictionary, offering etymologies, derivations, synonyms, usage notes, quotations and, for those languages where it’s relevant, declension and conjugation tables.
- [Online Etymology Dictionary](https://etymonline.com/) : the legendary ‘Etymonline’. An impressive dictionary mapping ‘wheel-ruts of modern English’. Trusted and reliable, the site offers dives into English words and explains how they evolved and where and when usages emerged.
- [RhymeZone](https://rhymezone.com/) : excellent site for searching not only for rhymes but also synonyms, homophones, phrase rhymes, conceptual relationships and more.

### **General Reference**

### **Politics and Media**

Although this site is not for the most part intended to focus too much on The Horrors, sometimes it's useful to know which is the most current and pressing of them.

Here, then, are a bunch of links to some of my favoured news sites, based both on the bias assessment from Media Bias Fact Check (see link below), as well as my own experience. That latter element is only to the exclusion of a couple of sites I'd otherwise have included: the **BBC**, because fuck their transphobic noise, I don't care how Fair And Balanced they are otherwise; and the **Guardian** for the same reason.

- [Full Fact](https://fullfact.org/)
- [Media Bias Fact Check](https://mediabiasfactcheck.com/) : before you go believing that thing that Gav told you down the pub which he knows FURRA FACT; or the damning statistics going round Facebook about how we need to PROTECT ARE BOARDERS FROM IMMERGRUNTS, look at where the claims are being published, and run them through this site. Search for news sources—newspapers, websites, TV, radio and online broadcasting—and check out their ownership (which billionaire do they profit?), assessments of their political lean (or outright bias) and the factuality of their reporting (or whether they just make shit up out of cloth).
- [Ground News](https://ground.news/) : No wait... I know you've seen it pushed on every YouTuber's channel for years and you're fed up of hearing about it, but (and I promise they're not sponsoring me; nobody is) the service is actually pretty useful. It's an aggregator, which identifies reports on the same story from across the political spectrum, and allows you to compare how they're being presented. This can help you pin down your own biases; as well ad giving you an idea of how people on the 'other side' are being influenced. Basic free access is available, and increasingly detailed analytics are added if you take one of the subscription plans.
